Our Vision & Mandate

Chosen People's Church Vision

"To heal the brokenhearted, the rejected and abandoned through the instrumentality of the word and sincere Love of GOD"— The Mandate

Healing & Restoration

We are called to minister healing and hope to the brokenhearted, bringing restoration to the rejected and abandoned in our community and beyond.

Instrumentality of the Word

Rooted in the truth, we teach and preach the Word of God as the primary instrument for transforming lives, renewing minds, and building strong foundations.

Sincere Love of God

We express the sincere love of God, creating an authentic, welcoming family where everyone is embraced, valued, and nurtured in Christ's love.
